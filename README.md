### Small tools for small-business websites, and a few hackathon builds

Most of what is here started from one scan of 43,000 small-business websites and
19,113 mail domains, and from the problems that kept showing up in it.

**Free tools**

- [Free website check](https://stephen-psaradellis.github.io/demos/site-check.html) - type a business website address and see, in plain English,
  what a customer or Google runs into on its home page (not secure, broken on phones, missing
  Google title, old year and more), each with a free step-by-step fix. No sign-up.
- [Email forgery check](https://stephen-psaradellis.github.io/demos/email-forgery-check.html) -
  type a domain, see whether anyone can send mail in its name (DMARC, SPF). Runs in your browser.
  Source: [email-forgery-check](https://github.com/Stephen-Psaradellis/email-forgery-check)
- [Mailwick Site Audit](https://github.com/Stephen-Psaradellis/mailwick-site-audit) - a WordPress plugin
  with seven plain-English checks (email forgery, SSL and mixed content, updates, phone view, tap-to-call,
  form mail, search snippet) in Tools > Site Health.
- [Local Site Audit + Pitch](https://github.com/Stephen-Psaradellis/local-site-audit) - a browser extension
  for web designers: one click on a business's site gives a client report of real defects with evidence,
  plus a pitch email written from them. [Get it on Edge Add-ons](https://microsoftedge.microsoft.com/addons/detail/hcdpnkilmailiiljmaeldcomceoibajh).
- [Keystone Lite](https://github.com/Stephen-Psaradellis/plumber-website-template) - a free one-page website template
  for local service businesses, one per trade: [plumber](https://github.com/Stephen-Psaradellis/plumber-website-template), [dentist](https://github.com/Stephen-Psaradellis/dentist-website-template),
  [cleaning](https://github.com/Stephen-Psaradellis/cleaning-website-template), [law firm](https://github.com/Stephen-Psaradellis/law-firm-website-template), [roofing](https://github.com/Stephen-Psaradellis/roofing-website-template), [electrician](https://github.com/Stephen-Psaradellis/electrician-website-template).
  Accessible, dark mode, no build step.
  [Preview](https://stephen-psaradellis.github.io/plumber-website-template/). The full 16-page Keystone is
  [$29 on Gumroad](https://localwebkit.gumroad.com/l/keystone).
- [The data story](https://stephen-psaradellis.github.io/demos/small-business-email-forgery-2026.html) -
  57.2% of small-business mail domains publish no DMARC record at all.

**Builds**

- [Loose Ends](https://github.com/Stephen-Psaradellis/loose-ends) - a hands-first WebXR evening wind-down:
  speak what is on your mind, sort it by pinch, voice or a look.
  [Try it](https://stephen-psaradellis.github.io/loose-ends/)
- [Owed](https://github.com/Stephen-Psaradellis/owed) - an invoice-collections agent on PayPal.
- [LineupLab](https://github.com/Stephen-Psaradellis/lineuplab) - plans an event lineup from taste data.
  [Live](https://lineuplab.s-n-psaradellis.workers.dev)
- [SiteKeeper](https://github.com/Stephen-Psaradellis/sitekeeper) - an owner files an issue, an agent ships
  the change to a small site, tests it live and rolls back on failure.

**Paid fixes**

If one of the checks above found something on your site, the fixes are written up with before and after
at [stephen-psaradellis.github.io/demos](https://stephen-psaradellis.github.io/demos/), each with a fixed price.
Agencies: [prospect packs](https://stephen-psaradellis.github.io/demos/prospect-packs.html) - local businesses
whose site is down or broken, with the evidence.

Written and built with an AI system (Claude) under my direction; I review and maintain it.
Issues and pull requests welcome on any repo.
